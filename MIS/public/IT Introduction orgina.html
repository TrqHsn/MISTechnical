🖥️ New User IT Orientation

Basic IT Guidelines for All Employees

1. 🔐 Password & Computer Security

🔑 Password Rules

  • Password must be 7–10 characters.
  • Must contain letters and numbers.
  • Change your password every 90 days.
  • You cannot reuse your last 15 passwords.
  • Windows will warn you 15 days before your password expires.

🔒 Account Lock

  • 3 wrong password attempts will lock your account.
  • If your account is locked, contact the MIS Team.

🔐 Lock Your Computer

  • Your computer will automatically lock after 15 minutes of inactivity.
  • Always lock your computer when you leave your seat.
  • Press Windows + L to lock your computer immediately.
💡 Remember

Never share your password with anyone.

Leaving your desk? → Press Windows + L

2. 🔄 Computer Shutdown & Restart

💻 Laptop Users

  • Do not use Sleep Mode. It is prohibited.
  • Shut down your laptop properly every day before leaving.
  • If your laptop becomes slow, restart it first.

🖥️ Desktop Users

Before leaving the office:

  1. Shut down your computer properly.
  2. Wait until the computer is completely off.
  3. Shut down your UPS.
  4. Switch off the UPS power.

❓ Why?

Because of frequent load shedding/power outages, leaving the UPS running unnecessarily can:

  • Cause your computer to have power-related problems.
  • Sometimes prevent the computer from booting properly.
💡 Remember

Laptop → No Sleep Mode → Shut down daily.

Desktop → Shut down PC → Shut down UPS → Switch off UPS power.

Computer slow? → Restart first.

3. ☁️ OneDrive & Files / Folders

🔄 Automatic Backup

These folders are automatically synced to OneDrive:

  • Desktop
  • Documents
  • Pictures
⚠️ Important
  • Downloads and other folders are not automatically synced.
  • Do not keep important company files only in Downloads or other unsynced folders.
  • Check that OneDrive has finished syncing before assuming your files are backed up.

🛠️ Before Sending Your Computer to MIS

Before giving your computer to MIS for repair, replacement, or maintenance:

Make sure all important files are backed up and fully synced to OneDrive.

👤 Personal Files

Do not store personal files, photos, videos, or other personal data on company computers or company OneDrive.

💡 Remember

Desktop + Documents + Pictures → OneDrive

Downloads + Other folders → Not automatically synced

5. 📧 Outlook & Email Storage

📮 Your Mailbox

  • Your Outlook mailbox has a fixed 50 GB storage limit.
  • The 50 GB limit cannot be increased.
  • Clean your mailbox regularly.
  • Do not wait until your mailbox is full.

🗑️ Deleted Emails

  • Most company accounts have a 1-month retention period for deleted emails.
  • Deleted emails may remain in Deleted Items for up to 1 month before being permanently removed.
  • Therefore, deleting an email may not immediately free storage.

📎 Large Emails & Attachments

  • Download important large attachments/files.
  • Save large files to OneDrive instead of keeping them in Outlook.
  • You have more than 1 TB of OneDrive storage available.

⚠️ Outlook Not Responding?

Save your work → Close Outlook → Restart your computer → Try again.

If the problem continues → Contact MIS.

💡 Remember

50 GB is the limit → Keep your mailbox clean.

Large file → Save it to OneDrive.

7. 🌐 Network Problems

📡 Network Not Working?

If you cannot access the internet, shared folders, or other network services:

  1. Unplug the network (LAN) cable from your computer.
  2. Unplug the other end of the cable.
  3. Wait a few seconds.
  4. Plug both ends back in firmly.
  5. Check the network again.

If It Still Doesn't Work

  • Do not change network settings.
  • Do not unplug other network cables.
  • Contact the MIS Team.
💡 Remember

Network problem → Reconnect the LAN cable → Check again → Contact MIS if still not working.

8. 💬 Microsoft Teams

📢 Official IT Communication Channel

Microsoft Teams is the official communication channel for IT support.

  • Contact the MIS Team through Microsoft Teams when required.
  • Do not use WhatsApp, Telegram, or other unofficial channels for IT support.
  • MIS will not be responsible for requests or information shared through unofficial channels.

🖥️ How to Share Your Screen

During a Teams call with MIS:

  1. Join the Teams call.
  2. Click Share.
  3. Select Screen or the required window.

🖱️ How to Give Control

If an authorized MIS Team member needs to control your screen:

  1. MIS will request control.
  2. Click Allow.
  3. MIS can then control your shared screen.
  4. Control can be stopped when the work is finished.
⚠️ Only give control to authorized MIS Team members.
💡 Remember

IT Support → Microsoft Teams

WhatsApp / Telegram → Not an official IT support channel

9. 🎣 Phishing Emails

Phishing emails are fake emails designed to trick you into giving away your password, information, or access.

⚠️ Be Careful If an Email:

  • Comes from outside the company and is marked in red text: “Caution: External Mail.”
  • Comes from an unknown or unexpected sender.
  • Asks for your password, login information, or sensitive data.
  • Contains a suspicious link or attachment.
  • Says “Urgent!”, “Act now!”, or tries to scare you.
  • Asks you to send company information.
  • Looks unusual or different from normal company emails.

👥 Know Your Team

Be familiar with your team and know who normally sends you emails.

If you receive a message from someone you do not recognize or would not expect to contact you, be careful.

🚫 Do Not

  • Do not share your password or sensitive information by email.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Do not open unexpected attachments.
  • Do not reply to unknown senders with confidential information.
  • Do not trust an email just because it has a company logo.

❓ If You Are Not Sure

STOP → Do not click → Contact the MIS Team.

If you accidentally click a suspicious link or open a suspicious attachment, contact MIS immediately.

💡 Remember

When in doubt, don't click. Ask MIS first.
